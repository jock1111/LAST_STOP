# Milestone A content and release notes

## Draft Roblox description

> The elevator only goes down. Enter LAST STOP with up to 11 others, clear impossible floors, and make it back before the doors close. Bank your haul to escape safely—or risk everything for one more stop.

## Original icon and thumbnail concepts

- **Icon:** close-up of a scratched elevator floor display reading `-01`, cyan glow against charcoal metal, with one small red warning light.
- **Thumbnail 1:** four players sprinting toward a cyan-lit elevator while a tall red-eyed silhouette stands in a dark archive aisle.
- **Thumbnail 2:** split decision scene: left panel gold `BANK`, right panel red `CONTINUE`, with an at-risk coin count between them.
- **Thumbnail 3:** flooded laboratory with blue reflective water, three glowing pump terminals, and the return elevator in the distance.

Use only art created by the team or assets whose Roblox usage rights have been verified. No asset IDs are asserted in this project.

## Content / community guidance for this slice

- Suggested experience disclosure: stylized suspense, darkness, monster pursuit, and mild non-graphic combat. Verify current Roblox maturity labels before publishing.
- Community rule: no harassment, hate speech, exploits, evasion of moderation, or intentionally trapping teammates. Report tools and server enforcement need implementation before public launch.
- Analytics: do not emit analytics until a privacy review specifies event names, retention, consent/configuration needs, and Roblox policy compliance.

## 30-day launch experiment plan (not yet instrumented)

1. **Days 1–3:** verify tutorial completion, first floor completion, and technical errors; fix blockers before increasing traffic.
2. **Days 4–10:** compare objective readability and first-run BANK/CONTINUE decisions; tune only after sufficient privacy-safe aggregate samples.
3. **Days 11–20:** test room weighting and depth reward caps; inspect disconnect and escape-miss rates for fairness issues.
4. **Days 21–30:** trial one cosmetic-only presentation after PurchaseService, receipt handling, policy validation, and explicit user-value review are complete.

No monetization, gambling mechanic, or paid power is implemented in Milestone A.
